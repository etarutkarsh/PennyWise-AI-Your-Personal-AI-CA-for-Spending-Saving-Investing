Chapter I  ·  The cost of convenience

Cash used to tell you
when to stop.

Opening your wallet, counting notes, handing over ₹500 for dinner — there was physical feedback. A slight reluctance. A moment of pause. Your body registered what your brain was spending.

Then UPI arrived. ₹847 on Swiggy — one tap. ₹1,299 on Amazon — two seconds. ₹680 at Starbucks — done. The friction vanished. And with it, your instinctive awareness of what you were actually spending.

This is not a failure of willpower. It is a failure of visibility.
