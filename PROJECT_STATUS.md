## Engine Roadmap (Next Sprints)

### Sprint 1 — Decision Memory Engine ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Record every decision: recommendation, evidence, user choice, review date, outcome, accuracy, lesson.
This is the memory of the Digital Twin. The moat.

### Sprint 2 — Behavioral Engine ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Learn: spending recovery, risk drift, goal discipline, impulse score, salary growth, financial habits.
Feeds Decision Engine with personalized context.

### Sprint 3 — Financial Digital Twin ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Continuous state model: Cash, Assets, Debt, Goals, Insurance, Tax, Investments, Career, Family, Life Events, Behavior, Decision History.
Not "profile autofill" — a living financial model.

### Sprint 4 — Scenario Engine ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Extract scenario generation from DecisionEngine into a reusable service.
Every feature (house, career, retirement) generates scenarios through one engine.
